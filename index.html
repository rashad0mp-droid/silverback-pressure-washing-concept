Questions

Straight answers.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Tim comes out to see the property and gives a clear price before any work starts.

Pressure washing or soft washing: what's the difference?

Pressure washing uses high pressure for hard surfaces like concrete. Soft washing uses low pressure and cleaning solution for siding, brick and roofs, so nothing gets damaged.

Can you get oil stains out of my driveway?

In many cases, yes. Tim has handled everything from tire marks to a diesel spill. Results depend on how old and deep the stain is.

Do you work with HOAs and property managers?

Yes. Silverback has washed full condo communities and common areas. Request a bid for a walkthrough.
